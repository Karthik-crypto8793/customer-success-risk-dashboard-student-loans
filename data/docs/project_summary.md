# Project Summary

## Title
Customer Success Risk Dashboard for Overseas Student Loans

## Background
Customer success teams in loan servicing environments often manage large volumes of customer interactions across multiple stages such as lead creation, document verification, sanction review, and disbursement.

## Problem Statement
Without a structured monitoring framework, it becomes difficult to identify customers who are at risk of delay, dissatisfaction, or dropout.

## Proposed Solution
A dashboard-driven approach using customer servicing data to track:
- stage-wise volume
- turnaround time
- customer complaints
- escalation frequency
- satisfaction levels
- country-wise trends

## Business Value
This solution can help leadership teams:
- identify bottlenecks
- reduce delays
- improve customer communication
- prioritize high-risk cases
- improve customer satisfaction

## Ideal Users
- Customer Success Leaders
- Operations Managers
- Quality Teams
- Banking Service Delivery Teams
